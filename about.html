About

What AI StackWatch 24 is

AI StackWatch 24 is a daily market-intelligence publication built around one picture: the six-layer map of the artificial-intelligence economy. Thirty-six companies and platforms sit on the map — the power generators and fuel-cell makers of Layer 1, the chip designers and the foundry of Layer 2, the clouds and data-center landlords of Layer 3, the model labs of Layer 4, the data platforms of Layer 5, and the applications of Layer 6. Every company gets its own page; every page is refreshed daily.

Data sources & delays

Market data comes from Yahoo Finance's end-of-day feed, pulled after the US close. The current snapshot was fetched 2026-10-21T23:22:38Z UTC and covers the session of October 21, 2026. Prices are delayed end-of-day closes, not real-time quotes. If a ticker cannot be fetched, its page says Data pending — we never estimate, interpolate or recycle a stale price. Five names on the map (xAI/Grok, Mistral AI, Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks) are private companies with no public price; their pages track role, funding and news instead.

Methodology

Day changes are computed from consecutive end-of-day closes. Sparklines show the last 30 sessions of real closes. The Daily Brief counts advancers and decliners across the 31 public names and attributes a 'why' only when a catalyst is confirmed in a linked source; otherwise it says so. Breakthrough stories are summarised in our own words from linked reporting — follow the source link for the full story.

The map

  • Layer 1 · Energy — Electricity and equipment — the power under every data center: Oklo, GE Vernova, Vistra, Bloom Energy, Constellation Energy, NextEra Energy
  • Layer 2 · Chips — Compute and memory — the silicon the models run on: NVIDIA, Broadcom, TSMC, AMD, Micron, ASML
  • Layer 3 · Cloud & Infrastructure — Data centers and networks — where the AI physically lives: AWS · Amazon, Microsoft, Oracle, Equinix, Vertiv, Arista Networks
  • Layer 4 · AI Models — The labs that create the frontier models: Google DeepMind · Alphabet, Grok · xAI (SpaceX), Mistral AI, Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI
  • Layer 5 · Platforms — Data and software — the layer businesses build on: Palantir, Snowflake, Salesforce, Adobe, ServiceNow, Databricks
  • Layer 6 · Applications — AI in the hands of individuals and businesses: UiPath, Duolingo, SoundHound AI, AppLovin, Roblox, Twilio
